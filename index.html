The Origin Story

The Legend of Bunty Kaka

In the vibrant streets of Bhavnagar, Bunty Kaka started with a simple handcart and a grandmother's treasured recipe. What began as a modest evening stall soon became a culinary landmark.

Every plate served carries the weight of generations — the perfect balance of crispy textures, bold spices, and unwavering dedication to quality. This isn't just food; it's a legacy passed down with love.
